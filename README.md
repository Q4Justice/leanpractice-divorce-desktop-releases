# LeanPractice Divorce: downloads

The desktop app for Florida uncontested dissolutions with children. This page holds the installers and
updates only.

**Mac:** open the `.dmg` and drag the app into **Applications**, then open it from Applications.
The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app. Close that message, open **System Settings ›
Privacy & Security**, scroll down and choose **Open Anyway** next to "LeanPractice Divorce",
then enter your Mac password. You only do this once.

**Windows:** run the setup file. No administrator rights are needed. If Windows says it protected your
PC, choose **More info › Run anyway**.

### What's new in 1.1.0

- Child support counts a child who is 18 and still in high school, expected to graduate before 19, once you
  mark the child in Case and parties (§61.30(1)(a)). Ages are now taken on the support start date, or today
  until you set one. This can change the support amount on a saved case.
- The 20% overnight test for the substantial time-sharing method uses the actual nights in the year. In a leap
  year a parent needs 74 of 366 overnights; 73 is no longer enough.
- Child support now notes two deviation factors from §61.30(11)(a): support above 55% of the paying parent's
  gross income, and a paying parent with some overnights but fewer than 20%.
- When support uses the substantial time-sharing method, the term sheet and the firm template say that a
  parent who stops exercising time-sharing creates a substantial change, with any modification retroactive
  (§61.30(11)(c)).
- Agreement terms: you can make the paying parent's claim to a child for taxes depend on being current on
  support (§61.30(11)(a)8). The term sheet and the firm template include the clause.
- The firm template has these two new paragraphs. You'll be offered the updated template; your own edits are
  kept.
- Income and expenses notes that public assistance is not income, and the deduction for other children's
  support now reads "court-ordered, actually paid." §61.30 was checked against the 2026 Florida Statutes.
- New Law tables page (the link under the worksheet steps, or Settings › About this copy). It shows the
  child support schedule, the percentages above $10,000, the poverty guideline and the alimony, child support
  and filing limits the calculations use, each with its source. Type a combined net income to find its row;
  "Copy schedule" pastes the schedule into a spreadsheet to compare with the statute. Nothing on it can be
  edited, and no calculation has changed.

## Updates

The app checks this page for new versions. When one is available it shows a banner; choose
**Install and restart**. Your cases are saved and backed up first, and stay where they are.

## Your data

Cases are saved as files on your own computer (Documents › LeanPractice Cases unless you choose another
folder in Settings). Nothing is sent to this page.
